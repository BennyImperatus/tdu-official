About the Unit

The operational pillars of the HCPD Training & Discipline Unit.

Core Mission

TDU serves as the educational backbone of the Harford County Police Department. We deliver modern, tactical training programs for cadets, and implement objective evaluations to filter and promote high-quality personnel.

Ethical Standard

We enforce strict compliance with department protocols. Our duty includes field inspections, training oversight, and administrative audits to ensure all police actions remain professional, respectful, and lawful.

Leadership Core

A good officer is a leader first. TDU fosters decision-making capabilities, active communication under stress, and crisis mitigation strategies, molding recruits into community-facing exemplars.

Operational Pillars

Our officers are defined by three simple principles. These values are drilled into every cadet during the primary phases of the Academy:

01

Integrity

Do what is right, always, especially when no one is watching.

02

Professionalism

Speak clearly, behave in a disciplined manner, and command authority respectfully.

03

Preparedness

Know the law, maintain tactical awareness, and act with decisive speed.

Minimum Enlistment Criteria

  • Active member in the Harford County Police Department
  • Rank of Officer (L3) or above in HCPD
  • No disciplinary warnings or bans within the last 30 days
  • Active in Discord and possess a working microphone
  • Understand basic radio codes and arrest protocols
